## 🏢 About Jaey Ride

### What is Jaey Ride?
**Jaey Ride** (also known as **JRIDE**) is a campus-focused ride-hailing and carpooling platform designed to provide safe, affordable, and reliable daily transportation for students, lecturers, staff, and visitors.

- **Core Hub & Launch Location**: Mulungushi University (Great North Road Main Campus, Town Campus, surrounding hostels like Chambeshi & Lusisi, and the Kabwe metropolitan area in Zambia).
- **Mission**: Eliminating long waits at bus stops and taxi ranks, making travel between campus, hostels, and town effortless, safe, and transparent.
- **Official Web App**: [https://jaeyride.site](https://jaeyride.site)
- **App Access**: Works on any smartphone browser (PWA for Android, iOS/iPhone, and laptops), plus a dedicated **Android APK** downloadable directly from the website.

## 🚗 How the Service Works

### 1. Ride Modes
1. **Shared Rides (Per-Seat / Carpool)**:
   - Budget-friendly option for students heading in the same direction.
   - You book a single seat and split the ride, making it very economical (e.g. Hostels to Main Gate, Campus to Town).
2. **Private Rides (Whole Car / "In Taka")**:
   - For passengers who want the entire vehicle to themselves for speed, privacy, luggage, or comfort.
   - Priced at 3.5 seats so the driver is compensated for not picking up additional passengers.

### 2. Payment Methods
- **Cash**: Pay the driver directly in Zambian Kwacha (ZMW) when you arrive.
- **Mobile Money**: Direct peer-to-peer transfer to the driver via **Airtel Money** or **MTN Mobile Money** upon arrival.
- *No credit card or online payment gateway required.*

### 3. Safety & Driver Verification
- Every driver is verified before approval (checking driver’s license, vehicle fitness, and registration plate).
- Real-time GPS tracking during active rides.
- Both students and drivers can call and message each other inside the app.
- Transparent rating system: Students give drivers a 1 to 5 star rating after every completed ride.

## 💬 WhatsApp Customer Service FAQ (Quick Answers for the AI)

### Q1: "How do I book a ride?"
> "It's quick and easy! You can book right away from your phone:
> 1. Open your browser and visit: **https://jaeyride.site**
> 2. Enter your name and phone number.
> 3. Select your pickup location and where you want to go.
> 4. Choose **Shared** (per seat) or **Private** (whole car) and tap **Request Ride**!
> You'll see your assigned driver, their car plate number, and track them live on the map."

### Q2: "Where can I download the Android app?"
> "You can download the Android APK directly by visiting **https://jaeyride.site** on your phone and tapping **Download APK** on the home screen!
> If you are on an iPhone, simply open Safari, visit **https://jaeyride.site**, tap the Share button, and select **Add to Home Screen**."

### Q3: "How much does a ride cost?"
> "Our fares are student-friendly!
> - Common campus routes (like between Hostels, Main Gate, and Town) have cheap, fixed flat rates.
> - For other trips, fares are automatically calculated based on distance and travel time with transparent pricing before you confirm.
> - You can check the exact price upfront on the app before requesting!"

### Q4: "How do I become a driver?"
> "We'd love to have you on board! To register as a driver:
> 1. Visit **https://jaeyride.site** and toggle to **Driver Registration**.
> 2. Enter your details, car model, plate number, and upload your driver credentials.
> 3. Our admin team will review and approve your profile so you can start earning right away!"

### Q5: "What if I left an item in a car or have a complaint?"
> "We take your safety and belongings very seriously. Please send us your:
> - Full name
> - Phone number used for the ride
> - Trip date, time, and route
> - Description of the item or issue
> Our team (led by Reagan and support) will trace the ride record and assist you immediately!"
