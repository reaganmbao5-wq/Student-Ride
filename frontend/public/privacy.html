Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 13, 2026 • Official Data Protection Statement

1. Commitment to Privacy

Jaey Ride is dedicated to protecting your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we collect, how it is processed to deliver safe campus transport, and how your data is secured.

2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal data necessary for operating ride booking and safety tracking:

  • Account Credentials: Name, University Email address, Phone Number, and optional profile picture.
  • Real-Time Location Data: Precise GPS coordinates during active ride requests, driver navigation, and trip tracking.
  • Trip & Rating Records: Pickup points, dropoff destinations, trip timestamps, fares, and ratings.
  • Device Information: Operating system, hardware model, and push notification tokens for ride status updates.
3. Use of Location Data

High-accuracy GPS location data is essential for route optimization and safety monitoring:

  • Location coordinates are shared with your assigned driver or passenger solely during an active trip.
  • Location broadcasting automatically terminates once a trip is completed or when a driver goes offline.
  • You may manage or revoke location permissions at any time via device settings.
4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Protection

Jaey Ride strictly prohibits selling, renting, or leasing user data to third-party advertisers. Personal information is disclosed only to matched trip participants for active navigation or to campus security authorities when legally required during safety emergency investigations.

5. Data Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS in transit and encrypted data storage) to safeguard user credentials and trip history. Data is retained only as long as necessary to fulfill platform operations or legal compliance.

6. Your Rights & Account Deletion

Users possess full rights to view, update, or request complete deletion of their account data. Account deletion can be triggered anytime directly from the Profile Settings tab in the app, or by submitting a deletion request to support.

When you request account deletion, all personal data, including your name, email, phone number, vehicle details (for drivers), and profile records are permanently removed from our active database within 30 days, except where retention is strictly required by applicable legal or safety obligations.

7. Privacy Policy Updates

Jaey Ride reserves the right to update this Privacy Policy. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
